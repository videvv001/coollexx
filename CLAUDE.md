## Changelog policy

Every change made in this repo gets an entry in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md),
added under `## [Unreleased]` in the same commit/PR as the change (create the
`[Unreleased]` section if it's not there). Keep entries one line, user- or
developer-facing (what changed and why it matters), not a restatement of the
diff. Group under `### Added` / `### Changed` / `### Fixed` / `### Removed`
subheadings as needed. When a release is cut, rename `[Unreleased]` to the
version and date, and start a fresh empty `[Unreleased]` above it.
